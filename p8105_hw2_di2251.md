**Data-set description:** This data-set contains information related to
each entrance and exit for each subway station in New York City. As a
part of the initial data cleaning, the variable names of the imported
data were automatically reformatted to a standardized format. The 19
variables or columns relevant to the problem were selected out of the 32
total variables (columns) in the original data-set. These selected
variables contain information on the subway line, station name, station
latitude/longitude, route number, entry, vending, entrance type, and ADA
compliance. There are a total of 1,868 rows or observations in the
data-set. In addition, the entry variable was converted from a character
variable of `YES` vs `NO` to a logical variable. However, this data is
not tidy since the lines that run or do not run through each of routes 1
to 11 are independently displayed the information can be collapsed into
a character variable listing which lines travel along which routes
rather than having them all listed separately.

The baseline demographics data were imported from `MCI_baseline.csv`.
The first row of the file contained variable descriptions rather than
column names, so it was skipped during import (`skip = 1`), and missing
values, which were coded as `.` in the raw file, were converted to `NA`.
Column names were then cleaned to snake case. The dataset contains 6
variables: participant ID, age at baseline (`current_age`), sex, years
of education, APOE4 carrier status, and age at onset of MCI
(`age_at_onset`). Sex and APOE4 status were originally coded numerically
(1 = male, 0 = female; 1 = carrier, 0 = non-carrier) and were recoded
into descriptive character labels. A missing value for age at onset
indicates that the participant remained MCI-free during follow-up.

In total, 483 participants were recruited. Participants whose age at
onset was less than or equal to their baseline age already had MCI at
baseline and did not meet the inclusion criteria, so 4 participants were
removed, leaving 479 participants in the analytic dataset. Of these, 93
developed MCI during follow-up. The average baseline age was 65 years,
and 30% of women in the study were APOE4 carriers.
